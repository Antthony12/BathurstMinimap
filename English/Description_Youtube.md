🏁 Bathurst Minimap for GTA V — full lap around Bathurst

In this video I take a full lap of Bathurst in GTA V using my Bathurst Minimap mod, a script that displays a live map on screen with your position over the real circuit — just like the classic Nürburgring mod, but adapted to this track using real telemetry recorded on the actual circuit.

🔧 About the mod
- Map with the real Bathurst layout, generated from thousands of telemetry points recorded while completing a lap of the circuit.
- Live pin that follows your position on the map in real time.
- The map appears automatically only when you are on the circuit and disappears when you leave it.
- Map position, size, and detection distance fully configurable from an .ini file.
- Open source (MIT).
